Ground rules

Do
  • Blue #4C7DFF for primary actions & active nav only.
  • Status colors carry meaning — never as decoration.
  • Reference every color through a token, never a raw hex.
  • Space Grotesk for headings & numbers; Inter for everything else.
Don't
  • Introduce a second accent hue per surface.
  • Mix radii — stay on the 6 / 12 / 16 / 20px scale.
  • Let one site drift its own look — evolve the system for all three together.
  • Hardcode greys outside the token definitions.
